## What this page establishes

The same four time windows appear here as on a member's own record page
(raw/pages/memberhome_action-viewrecords_reqid-3.md), and the window shown is the current
term.

Unlike Service Hours, the Meetings requirement offers no free-text entry form. Instead the
page states in words that meeting credit has to come from an event, either by logging a
past meeting or by adding the member to a meeting's attendee list. So the product does
restrict manual credit entry on some requirements and not others.

The word "Officer" printed next to the member's name is their account type.
