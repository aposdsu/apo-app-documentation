Billing rules, verbatim:
"Payment for APOonline is billed once per term for the entire chapter at the end of each
 term to cover all months since last invoice."
"The number of members charged is calculated at the time of invoice (half way through
 each term)."
"Advisor, Honorary, Inactive, and Alumni members are not included in this count (they are
 free)."

This explains the "At Cost?" column on ?action=managestatuses: it marks which account
types the vendor charges for.

The document manager page (?action=viewstorage) is blocked with
"In order to use the document management feature, you must upgrade your APOonline
 subscription to the Diamond level." and "Only the administrator for this APOonline
 subscription can upgrade, usually this is only the President." The event form likewise
shows "Diamond Subscription Required" next to Mobile Check-in. So some features are
gated by the vendor's plan, not by chapter permissions.
